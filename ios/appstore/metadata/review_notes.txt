entropybrush is a self-contained painting app. No account or login is required and it collects no data — everything runs on-device.

To try it:
- Pick a color from the palette along the bottom.
- Drag one finger on the canvas to paint a stroke; tap for a single dab.
- Two fingers pan and pinch-zoom the canvas.
- The circular gizmo (top-right) tilts the canvas in 3D; drag it while painting to rotate the view.
- The spin control flings wet paint outward for spin-art.

iPad only.
