Privacy Policy

entropybrush · last updated 8 September 2026

entropybrush does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything you make stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. entropybrush has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind. It contains no third-party data-collection SDKs, and it does not send any information about you or your device to us or to anyone else.

Your artwork

The paintings you create live entirely on your device. When you export a painting as an image or a 3D model, that file is saved or shared only where you choose to put it — it is never uploaded to us.

Children

entropybrush is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
